SAMPLE ITINERARY

48 hours on the Cape Coast.

Base yourself in Cape Coast for a weekend that takes you from the Atlantic shore to the forest canopy at Kakum.

  1. Saturday

    Cape Coast Castle & Elmina

    Start with a guided visit to Cape Coast Castle to learn about its role in the transatlantic slave trade. Spend the afternoon by the water in Elmina.

  2. Sunday

    Kakum National Park

    Head inland for the canopy walk, then return to Cape Coast for lunch before heading home.

Before you go.

Can I plan a trip across several regions?

Yes. Start with where you’ll depart and list the places you’d like to visit in your trip brief. Include your dates so you can plan time for travel between stops.

Can I start without choosing a destination?

Yes. Choose “Help me decide” in the planner, then tell us what you enjoy. The destination guides offer starting points for a weekend by the sea, a city break or time outdoors.

Does the trip brief make a booking?

The brief organizes your preferences so you can save and share them. It leaves transport, accommodation and other reservations open for you to arrange.
